Do you want to build a partnership? Disney meets Vail.

Role partnership strategy, market analysisTeam fourFormat strategic recommendation

the setup

Disney was facing softening demand and post-COVID normalization in its Parks and Experiences division, alongside a planned $60B investment in it. Our team proposed something Disney doesn't have: winter. A partnership with Vail Resorts, the largest ski operator in the world with 42 resorts and over 36% market share, to bring Disney storytelling to the slopes.

the case

Two target markets: millennials and Gen Z chasing shareable experiential travel, and middle-income families wanting an immersive winter vacation. Branded lodging, themed dining, character experiences on the mountain, and ski-school partnerships, all bundled with Disney Parks to smooth seasonality for both companies. Vail gets Disney's brand and storytelling; Disney gets a year-round calendar and a market it has never touched.

Disney's problem was seasonality and saturation. Vail's was brand and reach. A partnership solves both with less capital than either could deploy alone.

what we built

Customer segmentation, competitive analysis, a cost overview, a phased timeline starting with pilot locations to limit risk, a marketing plan, and the performance metrics that would tell Disney whether to expand. It was a fun one to present. Elsa's Ice Castle is a real line in the deck.
