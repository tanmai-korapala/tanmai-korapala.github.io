the work stuff.

I am a growth strategist.

I like finding the real problem underneath the stated one, and building the plan that fixes it.

right now

I'm the Growth & Strategy Manager at DBaasNow, an early-stage B2B database platform, where I've been since late 2023. The most important thing I did there was ask why a $68.6M market had produced zero customers. Mapping adoption barriers across nine competitors led to a five-year plan the C-suite adopted and now uses to run pitch and demo calls. Along the way I built the company's first marketing strategy (impressions up 118% in six weeks), built the CRM from scratch and generated 500+ leads, and redesigned the acquisition funnel with a team spread across five countries, which grew product engagement more than 150%.

before that

I spent almost three years at General Motors as a District Sales Manager, covering Utah, Arizona, and Wyoming, then Wichita, then Little Rock. The title says sales, but the job was working with the 48 franchised dealerships on identifying growth opportunities, especially underperformance, and getting them to their goals. The UT/AZ/WY district climbed from 136th to 66th out of 185 nationally, revenue grew 20%, margins grew 9%, and the performance tracking framework I built lifted dealer market share 5% and annual vehicle sales 12%. I presented to 50+ dealer principals and corporate leaders and learned that a plan only counts if the people on the ground can run it.

consulting

During my MBA I worked on six engagements. The biggest was a capstone with Wayfair, where a team of four of us found a projected $1.8M cost-savings opportunity across 13 nodes of their small-parcel returns network and built the roadmap to capture it. For Wise Street Capital, a fintech venture, I turned a $24B unclaimed retirement benefits market into an investor-ready pitch. For Pine Point Medical, a local clinic, I built a bottom-up model that quantified a $1.3M profit opportunity and a pricing strategy that lifted DEXA scan bookings 20%. I also advised the Ithaca Health Alliance board on $500K+ in annual donations, and built a go-to-market strategy for a Hershey product launch that hit 71% purchase intent in testing.
