Wise Street Capital · Strategy & Market Research Consultant · summer 2025

Wise Street Capital: turning a $24B problem into a pitch.

Role market sizing, segmentation, competitive researchClient pre-seed fintech founderOutcome investor-ready market case

the problem

Every year, Americans leave roughly $24 billion in employer 401(k) match on the table, mostly because the rules are confusing and nobody reminds you before the deadline. Wise Street Capital was an early-stage fintech idea aimed squarely at that: a simple tool that tells an individual exactly how much to contribute and when. The founder had the concept and the patents. What he didn't have was the market story an investor would need to hear.

what i did

I built the market case from the ground up. Top-down sizing from the total addressable market to the serviceable and obtainable slices, with the assumptions written out so an investor could argue with them. Customer segmentation that identified who actually loses match money and why: young professionals new to 401(k) decisions, mid-career employees who think they're on track, and job changers who lose match in the transition. And competitive benchmarking across more than twenty firms, which showed that nearly all of them sold to employers and plan sponsors, leaving the individual with nothing built for them.

The competitive map was the insight. Everyone was building for HR departments. Nobody was building for the person actually losing the money.

what i delivered

A structured set of recommendations for the CEO on positioning, target segments, and the go-to-market sequence, packaged into the market section of an investor brief. Working directly with a solo founder on a pre-product idea is the purest version of strategy work: there's no data to hide behind, so the logic has to hold on its own.
