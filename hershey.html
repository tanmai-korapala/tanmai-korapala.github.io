Cornell Johnson · Battle of the Brands · fall 2024

Hershey: launching Shaq-A-Licious gummies.

Role go-to-market strategy, segmentation, pricingTeam sixOutcome 71% purchase intent in testing

the brief

Battle of the Brands is a Johnson competition where teams take a real product and build the go-to-market strategy, then pitch it at a live marketplace. Ours was Shaq-A-Licious, Hershey's Shaquille O'Neal-branded gummy line, positioned as the "sweet power of play."

what we did

We started with primary research: a survey on where people buy candy, how old they are, and whether they even know who Shaq is. That produced two segments that wanted very different things. Late-night convenience-store buyers in their twenties who want novelty and a reason to grab something fun. And nostalgic grocery shoppers in their mid-thirties who rarely buy sweets but grew up watching Shaq, and will make an impulse purchase on memory alone.

Same gummy, two completely different reasons to buy. The strategy was to stop pretending there was one customer.

what we built

A convenience-store channel strategy as the primary launch, pricing analysis, and a marketplace activation built around a basketball hoops challenge with discounts for winners, plus nostalgic '90s imagery for the older segment. In testing, the strategy achieved 71% purchase intent. And yes, I now know more about sour gummy flavor architecture than I ever expected to.
